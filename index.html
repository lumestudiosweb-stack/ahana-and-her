Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Ahana & Her offer home visits?

Yes — all of our therapy and care is delivered at your home across Mangalore, so you can heal in a familiar, comfortable environment.

What services does Ahana & Her provide?

We provide physiotherapy, occupational therapy, clinical psychology, speech therapy, behavioural therapy, nutrition counselling, and child development support.

Which areas does Ahana & Her serve?

We provide home-visit care across Mangalore, where we also run a Cold Plunge & Physio Recovery Unit and support the local running community.

How do I book an appointment with Ahana & Her?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 73589 69579 (Dr. Smrithi) and we will arrange a convenient home visit.

Who are the founders of Ahana & Her?

Ahana & Her was co-founded by Smrithi Natanasubramanian, an occupational therapist, and Viswa Laxmi Venugopal, a physiotherapist.

Do you offer child development and paediatric therapy?

Yes — we offer dedicated child development and early-intervention support tailored to every milestone.
