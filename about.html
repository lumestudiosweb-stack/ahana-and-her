The Name

Why Ahana?

The name was chosen with profound deliberateness. Ahana (ಅಹಣ) in Kannada means the fact of coming into existence — a word that captures everything this organisation stands for.

When a person accesses the right care, something transforms. They step out of pain, out of isolation, out of the shadows of unmet need — and into themselves. That emergence is Ahana.

And "Her" makes it personal. This is not healthcare in the abstract — it is care built around the human at its centre, by people who understand.

"A metaphor for personal transformation, societal renewal, and resilience."

The Symbol

The Phoenix
in our wings

The phoenix wings in our logo are not decorative — they are a declaration. The phoenix is one of humanity's oldest symbols of rebirth, strength, and transformation.

For Ahana & Her, the phoenix represents the journey every person takes when they choose to prioritise their health and wellbeing.
